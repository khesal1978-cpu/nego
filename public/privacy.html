Privacy Policy

Updated 24 October 2026

SIXGUN is a browser game by ArsDev. This policy explains what the game uses to save your preferences and run online matches.

What stays on your device

Your player name, character choices, settings, tutorial completion and unlocked progress are saved in your browser. These saves are tied to the website address and browser you use. You can remove them by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings; doing so resets your saved setup.

Online matches

When you open Custom Multiplayer, the multiplayer service receives a temporary player ID and your display name and character choices. Room names, codes and availability may appear in the public room list. Players in your room receive the information needed to play, including movement, actions and scores. Use a nickname instead of personal information. Room state is temporary; abandoned rooms expire. Solo play does not open a multiplayer connection.

Microphone and connections

Voice chat uses your microphone only after you enable it and grant browser permission. Audio is sent to other room participants, directly or through a relay. SIXGUN does not make voice recordings; fallback relay packets may be buffered temporarily in the database and expire automatically. You can switch the microphone off or revoke permission at any time. Direct peer connections can expose your network address to other participants; connection services also process network addresses to route traffic.

Service providers

The deployment uses Vercel for the website and multiplayer functions, MongoDB Atlas for temporary room coordination and fallback relay packets, and connection services such as Cloudflare when a relay is configured. Google Fonts supplies interface fonts. These providers may process IP addresses, browser information and operational or security logs under their own policies. SIXGUN does not include advertising trackers or a gameplay analytics service. Clicking the YouTube credit opens YouTube, which has its own privacy policy.

Your choices and updates

You can play solo without enabling voice, leave online rooms, revoke microphone access or clear your local saves. Do not share sensitive information in a room or voice chat. This policy may change when game features or providers change; the date above identifies the latest version. For privacy questions, visit ArsDev’s linked channel.
