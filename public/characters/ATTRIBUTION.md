# Motu and Patlu models

Motu: **Motu patlu** by **Romeo_e** — https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/motu-patlu-26350fcd9da94bad9d14c832a97808c5

Patlu: **Motu Patlu** by **shreyhaldkar0** — https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/motu-patlu-b496a23255634b70ad0453528ac0d5bd

Both source files identify their license as **CC BY 4.0**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Adaptations: normalized size and orientation, complete supplied FBX skeletons and blended skin weights, game animation controls, relaxed arms with standing hand clearance, compact reduced preview/match surfaces, smoothed normals, brighter cel shading, skinned ink outlines and rendered portraits. Original texture atlases retained. In-game attribution appears in Settings → About.

Regenerate geometry and textures with `node scripts/import-motu-patlu.mjs <downloads-directory>`. The importer reads `Motu.fbx`, `motu/Motu_diff.png`, `motu-patlu/source/Patlu_global_ctrl.fbx` and the diffuse PNGs under `motu-patlu/textures/`.
